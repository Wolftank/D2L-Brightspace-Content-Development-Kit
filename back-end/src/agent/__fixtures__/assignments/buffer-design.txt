Lab Report 4: Designing a Buffer

Due Friday at 5 pm. Worth 20 points.

Why you are doing this
This report practises the skill chemists use most outside the lab: choosing a
method and defending the choice with data. You will use it again in the
capstone project and in any job that asks you to justify a decision.

What to do
1. Choose a weak acid from the list on the lab handout and calculate the
   ratio of acid to conjugate base for a buffer at pH 5.0.
2. Make the buffer in the lab and measure its pH three times.
3. Write a report of no more than four pages with an introduction, your
   calculation, your measurements in a table, and a discussion.
4. Submit it to the dropbox as a PDF.

How it will be graded
A strong report:
- shows the Henderson-Hasselbalch calculation with units at every step (6 points)
- reports all three measurements and their mean in a labelled table (4 points)
- explains any difference between the target and measured pH with at least
  one specific source of error (6 points)
- is clearly written and no longer than four pages (4 points)
